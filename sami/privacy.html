Sami — privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Sami is a private household assistant, run by the owner of this site for his own household. It is not offered to the public.

What it reads

Calendar events and Google Drive files that the household explicitly shares with the assistant's own Google account. Access is read-only.

Why

Only to answer the household's own questions.

Where the data goes

  • The Google access token is stored encrypted on a private home server.
  • To answer a question, the relevant content is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for processing.
  • Conversation history is kept on the same private server.

Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or stop sharing the calendar or folder.

Contact

Through the site owner's GitHub profile.
